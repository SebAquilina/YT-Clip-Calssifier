Candle Defects You Can Fix WITHOUT Repouring — In 30 Seconds Each

Don't melt it down and start over! These are the quick surface fixes that rescue a flawed candle in about 30 seconds each — no repouring, no waste. Leaning wicks, sinkholes, rough tops, tunneling, loose toppings, long sooty wicks, wet spots, and crooked labels — all fixable with a heat gun, scissors and a wick bar.

You'll learn:
• Recenter a wick that set crooked
• Melt out a sinkhole for a flat top
• Smooth a rough surface to a glassy finish
• Level a tunneled candle
• Lock loose botanicals in place
• Tame a long, smoky flame
• Clear wet spots on the glass
• Straighten a crooked label
