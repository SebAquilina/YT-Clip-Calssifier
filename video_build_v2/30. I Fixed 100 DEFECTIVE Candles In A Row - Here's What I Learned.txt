I Fixed 100 DEFECTIVE Candles In A Row — Here's What I Learned

Last month one hundred candles came out of my workshop with something wrong with every single one — sunken tops, frosted sides, drowned wicks. Instead of binning a whole month of work, I fixed all one hundred by hand, and it taught me more than ten years of getting it right. Here are the ten defects I kept finding and exactly how I fixed each one — from wet spots and sinkholes to weak scent and tunneling.

Tell me in the comments which defect you've battled with. I read every single one. Happy candle making!
