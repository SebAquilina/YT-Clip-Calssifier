Candle Defects You Can Fix WITHOUT Repouring — In 30 Seconds Each

Before you melt a candle down and start over, try this. After 15 years of pouring, here are 10 candle defects you can fix in about 30 seconds each — with nothing but a heat gun, wick scissors and a wick bar. No repouring, no wasted wax.

Fixes covered:
• Leaning wick — warm & recenter
• Sinkhole — melt the top level
• Rough/lumpy top — heat-gun smooth
• Tunneling — melt the ring flat
• Loose toppings — set botanicals in
• Long sooty wick — trim to 1/4 inch
• Wet spots — warm the glass to re-adhere
• Frosting — light surface pass
• Wick mushrooming — snip the carbon bulb
• Surface cracks — re-flow the top
