✅ THE REAL SCENT-THROW CHECKLIST
1. Weigh fragrance to your wax's rated load (≈8–10% for most soy) — don't exceed it.
2. Add fragrance at ~185°F and stir gently for a full 2 minutes.
3. Right-size your wick for a full, edge-to-edge melt pool.
4. Cure the candle at least 2 weeks before you judge it.

Do those four things and your throw transforms — no extra oil required.
