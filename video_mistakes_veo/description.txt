📖 New to candles? My book "The $3 Luxury Candle" walks beginners through clean, non-toxic soy & beeswax candles for pennies — the beginner-mistakes chapter covers everything here: https://candicescandles.com

Almost every beginner ruins their first candle the same few ways — sinkholes, tunneling, frosting, a drowned wick, too much fragrance, and the big one nobody warns you about. In this video I walk through the biggest first-candle mistakes and exactly how to dodge each one, using one simple starter kit that makes about five candles: what's in the kit, melting the wax (don't stress the temperature), adding dye and fragrance, pouring and topping up, trimming wicks, and the pretty petal-topped finish. Plus frosting & wet spots explained, jar choice, scent throw, gifting, safety, troubleshooting, and why patience (curing) matters most.
